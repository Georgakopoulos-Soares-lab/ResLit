                     CMY-21. This is the first study of plasmid-mediated AmpC genes in E. coli in the UK. Eleven cefoxitin-resistant, AmpC PCR-negative isolates had ampC promoter region mutations. All bla
                     CTX-M-15 and 24 of 25 bla
                     CMY genes were associated with an ISEcp1-like element. The bla
                     CTX-M-2 was located in an orf513-bearing class 1 integron. Plasmid restriction digests suggest transfer of genes between different plasmid backbones.
