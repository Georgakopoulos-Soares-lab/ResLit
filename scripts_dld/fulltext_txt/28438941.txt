## ABSTRACT
ABSTRACT Whole-genome sequencing of Staphylococcus xylosus strain JW2311 from bovine mastitis milk identified the novel 49.3-kb macrolide-lincosamide-streptogramin B (MLS B ) resistance plasmid pJW2311. It contained the macrolide resistance gene mph (C), the macrolide-streptogramin B resistance gene msr (A), and the new MLS B resistance gene erm (48) and could be transformed into Staphylococcus aureus by electroporation. Functionality of erm (48) was demonstrated by cloning and expression in S. aureus .
