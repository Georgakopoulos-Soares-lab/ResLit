## title
[Skin changes in art. I. Examples from the Germanistic National Museum in Nürnberg].

## abstract
At three works of art in possession of the Germanic National Museum, Nürnberg, the attempt was made of diagnosing dermatologic alternations. The three paintings show naevi pigmentosi, basaliomas and varicosis.
