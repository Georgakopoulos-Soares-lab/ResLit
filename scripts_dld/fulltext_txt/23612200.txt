## ABSTRACT
An NDM-1 carbapenemase-producing Pseudomonas aeruginosa isolate was recovered from a patient hospitalized in France after a previous hospitalization in Serbia. Genetic studies revealed that the bla NDM-1 gene was surrounded by insertion sequence IS Aba125 and a truncated bleomycin resistance gene. This bla NDM-1 region was a part of the variable region of a new complex class 1 integron bearing IS common region 1 (IS CR1 ). The presence of IS Pa7 upstream of this integron suggests insertion in a chromosomally located Tn 402 -like structure.
