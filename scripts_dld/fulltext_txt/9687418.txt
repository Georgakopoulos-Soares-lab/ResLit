Title: Tn5706, a transposon-like element from Pasteurella multocida mediating tetracycline resistance.

## ABSTRACT
The 4,378-bp putative tetracycline resistance transposon Tn 5706 of Pasteurella multocida consists of an internal tet (H)- tetR resistance gene region which is flanked by almost identical insertion elements, IS 1596 and IS 1597 . Two reading frames for proteins of 70 and 228 amino acids were detected in each of these insertion sequence elements. The 228-amino-acid protein revealed homology to transposase proteins of gram-positive bacteria.
