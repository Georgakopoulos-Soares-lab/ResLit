Title: pJIE137 carrying blaCTX-M-62 is closely related to p271A carrying blaNDM-1.
Journal: Antimicrobial agents and chemotherapy

Authors: Partridge SR, Paulsen IT, Iredell JR
Source: PMC_EFetch_XML
License: pmc_free_to_read

## ABSTRACT
Complete sequencing of pJIE137 revealed a backbone closely related to p271A, encoding a novel RepA protein but with a similar organization and up to ∼70% nucleotide identity to IncN plasmids. A region in pJIE137 resembling the IncN CUP regulon is mostly missing from p271A, presumably due to recombination. The class 1 In/Tn and IS Ecp1-bla CTX-M-62 transposition unit in pJIE137 and a putative transposon carrying bla NDM-1 in p271A are inserted in different locations in the plasmid backbone.
