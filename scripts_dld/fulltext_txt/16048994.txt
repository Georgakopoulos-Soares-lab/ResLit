Title: Integron-encoded GES-type extended-spectrum beta-lactamase with increased activity toward aztreonam in Pseudomonas aeruginosa.

## ABSTRACT
A Pseudomonas aeruginosa strain expresses an extended-spectrum β-lactamase, GES-9, which differs from GES-1 by a Gly243Ser substitution, is inhibited by clavulanic acid and imipenem, and hydrolyzes aztreonam. The bla GES-9 gene was located inside a class 1 integron structure containing two copies of a novel insertion sequence belonging to the IS 1111 family.
