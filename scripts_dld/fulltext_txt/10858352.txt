Title: Genetic organization of the downstream region of the mecA element in methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus isolates carrying different polymorphisms of this region.
Journal: Antimicrobial agents and chemotherapy
PubDate: 2000 Jul
Authors: Oliveira DC, Wu SW, de Lencastre H
Source: PMC_EFetch_XML
License: pmc_free_to_read

## ABSTRACT
We describe here the genetic organization of the mec element downstream of the mecA gene in 34 different methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA) clinical isolates carrying 13 of the most frequent polymorphisms of mecA and representing the major epidemic clones of MRSA. All polymorphisms carried three common genetic elements: the hypervariable region, a copy of IS 431 , and a unique 2-kb sequence (downstream constant segment, or dcs ) for which no homologous sequences are found in data banks. Polymorphisms of the downstream region were shown to be caused by the presence of linearized plasmids flanked by insertion sequences (pUB110, pT181, and pI258) and the autonomous insertion sequence IS 256 .
