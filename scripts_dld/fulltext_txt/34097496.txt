Title: Identification of a Novel Plasmid-Mediated Carbapenemase-Encoding Gene, bla(VMB-2), in Vibrio diabolicus.
Journal: Antimicrobial agents and chemotherapy
PubDate: 2021 Jul 16
Authors: Liu M, Zhang W, Peng K, Wang Z, Li R
Source: PMC_EFetch_XML
License: pmc_free_to_read

## ABSTRACT
ABSTRACT Here, we characterized a carbapenem-resistant Vibrio diabolicus strain of shrimp origin with various experiments and bioinformatics analysis. A novel metallo-β-lactamase (MBL) gene, bla VMB-2 , that confers resistance to β-lactams, including meropenem and cephalosporins, was identified on a plasmid-borne composite transposon, IS Shfr9 -IS CR1 - bla VMB-2 - bla CARB-12 - aadA1- IS Shfr9 , which is capable of generating a bla VMB-2 -bearing circular intermediate. IS Shfr9 was found to be disseminated in multidrug-resistant (MDR) pathogens, arousing the concern of further transmission of a bla VMB-2 -bearing circular intermediate to clinical Enterobacterales strains via such insertion sequences, which warrants further investigations.
