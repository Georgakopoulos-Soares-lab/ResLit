Title: Induction of L1 and L2 beta-lactamase production in Stenotrophomonas maltophilia is dependent on an AmpR-type regulator.
Journal: Antimicrobial agents and chemotherapy
PubDate: 2008 Apr
Authors: Okazaki A, Avison MB
Source: PMC_EFetch_XML
License: pmc_free_to_read

## ABSTRACT
A divergently oriented ampR has been located upstream of bla L2 in Stenotrophomonas maltophilia . AmpR is necessary for L1 and L2 β-lactamase induction in response to β-lactam challenge, and activation of AmpR is sufficient to induce L1 and L2 production. L1 induction requires more activation of AmpR than does L2 induction.
