Title: New transposon Tn6133 in methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus ST398 contains vga(E), a novel streptogramin A, pleuromutilin, and lincosamide resistance gene.
Journal: Antimicrobial agents and chemotherapy
PubDate: 2011 Oct
Authors: Schwendener S, Perreten V
Source: PMC_EFetch_XML
License: pmc_free_to_read

## ABSTRACT
A novel streptogramin A, pleuromutilin, and lincosamide resistance determinant, Vga(E), was identified in porcine methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA) ST398. The vga (E) gene encoded a 524-amino-acid protein belonging to the ABC transporter family. It was found on a multidrug resistance-conferring transposon, Tn 6133 , which was comprised of Tn 554 with a stably integrated 4,787-bp DNA sequence harboring vga (E). Detection of Tn 6133 in several porcine MRSA ST398 isolates and its ability to circularize suggest a potential for dissemination.
