Title: Molecular analysis of metallo-beta-lactamase gene bla(SPM-1)-surrounding sequences from disseminated Pseudomonas aeruginosa isolates in Recife, Brazil.

## ABSTRACT
The spread of clonally related carbapenem-resistant Pseudomonas aeruginosa producing the metallo-β-lactamase SPM-1 was found in Recife, Brazil. Upstream of bla SPM-1 , a novel common region (CR4) was identified, comprising an open reading frame, orf495 , whose product shares significant identity with putative recombinases, such as Orf513. CR4 may be responsible for mobilization and expression of bla SPM-1 .
