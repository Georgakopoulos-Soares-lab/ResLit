Title: New variant of CTX-M-type extended-spectrum beta-lactamases, CTX-M-71, with a Gly238Cys substitution in a Klebsiella pneumoniae isolate from Bulgaria.
Journal: Antimicrobial agents and chemotherapy
PubDate: 2009 Oct
Authors: Schneider I, Queenan AM, Markovska R, Markova B, Keuleyan E, Bauernfeind A
Source: PMC_EFetch_XML
License: pmc_free_to_read

## ABSTRACT
A single K lebsiella pneumoniae strain isolated in a Bulgarian hospital was found to produce CTX-M-71, a new CTX-M variant characterized by one amino acid substitution from glycine to cysteine at position 238 in comparison to CTX-M-15. This exchange decreased the hydrolytic activity of the β-lactamase for cefotaxime, ceftazidime, and cefepime.
