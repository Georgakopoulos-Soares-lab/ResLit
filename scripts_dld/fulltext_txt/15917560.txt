Title: Isolation and expression of a novel molecular class D beta-lactamase, OXA-61, from Campylobacter jejuni.

## ABSTRACT
A novel β-lactamase gene, bla OXA-61 , from Campylobacter jejuni GC015 was cloned and its nucleotide sequence determined. bla OXA-61 encodes a protein of 257 amino acids in which the active-site STFK tetrad and conserved class D β-lactamase motifs YGN and KTG were identified. A conserved sequence upstream of bla OXA-61 is required for expression in Campylobacter .
