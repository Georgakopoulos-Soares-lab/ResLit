Title: Characterization of Tn916S, a Tn916-like element containing the tetracycline resistance determinant tet(S).

## ABSTRACT
We have characterized a transferable tetracycline resistance (Tc r ) element from a Streptococcus intermedius isolate. The gene responsible for this resistance was identified by PCR and Southern hybridization as tet (S). Furthermore, the genetic support for this determinant was shown to be a conjugative transposon closely related to Tn 916 . This element has been designated Tn 916 S.
