Summary 

The aminoglycoside-3- O -acetyltransferase-I gene ( aacC1 ) from R plasmids of two incompatibility groups (R1033 [Tn 1696 ], and R135) was cloned and sequenced. In the case of R1033, it was shown that the aacC gene is coded by a precise insertion of 833 bp between the aadA promoter and its structural gene in a Tn 21 related transposon (Tn 1696 ). This insertion occurs at the same target sequence as that of the OXA-1 β-lactamase gene insertion in Tn 2603 . Upstream of the aacC gene, we found an open reading frame (ORF) which is probably implicated in the site-specific recombinational events involved in the evolution of this family of genetic elements. These results provide additional confirmation of the role of Tn 21 elements as naturally occurring interspecific transposition and expression casssettes.
