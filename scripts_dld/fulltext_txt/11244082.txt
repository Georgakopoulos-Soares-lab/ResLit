## ABSTRACT
We demonstrate that the Bacillus subtilis fosB(yndN) gene encodes a fosfomycin resistance protein. Expression of fosB requires ς W , and both fosB and sigW mutants are fosfomycin sensitive. FosB is a metallothiol transferase related to the FosA class of Mn 2+ -dependent glutathione transferases but with a preference for Mg 2+ and l -cysteine as cofactors.
