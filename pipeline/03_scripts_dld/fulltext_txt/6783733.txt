Abstract 

A Bacillus subtilis plasmid capable of producing phenotypic erythromycin resistance was compared with an Ery r staphylococcal plasmid. The two plasmids did not interfere with the sporulation process in B. subtilis , in contrast to chromosomal erythromycin mutations.
